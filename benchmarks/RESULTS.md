## Similarity search among all nodes

What a nearest-neighbour index over the vectors returns: the held-out neighbours of a query node are ranked among all nodes of the graph by dot product or cosine - whichever validates better for the method - with the known neighbours filtered out. `recall@k`, `ndcg@k`, `map@k`: of the top-k list of every query node. `all_nodes_mrr`: of every held-out edge.
